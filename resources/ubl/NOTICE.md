# Schemas

The XML schemas in this folder are included unchanged so that documents can be validated against them. They are not
covered by the package's MIT license; each keeps its own terms.

- Everything in `maindoc/` and `common/` except the Croatian extension: OASIS Universal Business Language (UBL) 2.1,
  Copyright (c) OASIS Open 2013, from http://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.1/. This includes OASIS's copies of the
  ETSI XAdES and W3C XML Signature schemas that come with the UBL 2.1 release.
- `common/HRExtensionAggregateComponents-1.xsd`: the Croatian extension (HRFISK20Data), published by the Croatian
  Tax Administration (Porezna uprava) with the e-invoice specification for fiscalization 2.0.
